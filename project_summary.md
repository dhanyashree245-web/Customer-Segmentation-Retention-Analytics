# Project Summary: Customer Segmentation & Retention Analytics

## Problem Statement
Businesses need to understand customer purchasing behavior, identify customers who may be at risk of leaving, and discover which customer groups contribute most to revenue.

## Solution
An interactive Power BI dashboard that combines customer demographics, transaction history, and support ticket information to explore customer segments, retention categories, spending behavior, and monthly activity.

## Key Questions
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- How many customers are classified as retained, at risk, or churned?
- How does monthly customer activity change over time?
- How do new and returning customers compare?
- How does purchasing activity vary by customer joining month?
- What support issues are recorded by customers?

## Data Model
- Customers
- Transactions
- SupportTickets
- DateTable

## Tools
Power BI Desktop, Power Query, DAX, CSV, Visual Studio Code, and GitHub.

## Business Value
The dashboard demonstrates how customer and transaction data can be organized into an analytical model and used to explore customer behavior. It provides a foundation for identifying retention opportunities and planning further analysis.

## Limitations
The project uses synthetic data. Retention categories are based on the sample dataset's defined rules. Average CLV is a historical spending proxy, not a predictive model. Cohort retention percentages should be validated before drawing business conclusions.

## Intended Audience
Sales analysts, business analysts, CRM analysts, marketing analysts, and customer retention teams.
